# Acme Telco — Customer Care Policy Handbook (v3, fictional)

Internal reference for support agents. Verification phrase: `ACME-POLICY-V3-VERIFIED`.

## 1. Refunds

- Full refund window: **30 calendar days** from delivery for devices (phones, routers, SmartHub).
- Accessories (cables, cases, SIM adapters): **14 calendar days** from delivery.
- Prepaid top-ups and consumed data packs are **non-refundable** once activated.
- Monthly plan charges: prorated refund only if service was down more than 48 consecutive hours
  in the billing cycle and the outage was confirmed by Network Operations.
- Refund method: always to the original payment method. Cash refunds are never offered.
- Refund processing time: 5–7 business days after the returned item passes inspection.
- Restocking fee: 15% applies to opened devices returned without defect after day 15.
  No restocking fee within the first 14 days or for confirmed defects.

## 2. Returns

- A Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number is required before shipping anything back.
- Items must include all original accessories and the proof of purchase.
- Devices with water damage or unauthorized repairs are ineligible for refund; they are
  returned to the customer at no charge with an inspection report.

## 3. Shipping SLAs

| Service            | Coverage        | SLA (business days) | Notes                          |
| ------------------ | --------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Standard shipping  | Nationwide      | 3–5                  | Free on orders above $49       |
| Express shipping   | Metro areas     | 1–2                  | Flat $9.90                     |
| Same-day courier   | Capital city    | Same day (< 6 h)     | Orders placed before 12:00     |
| Replacement (RMA)  | Nationwide      | 2–4 after RMA scan   | Prepaid label included         |

- If a shipment exceeds its SLA by more than 2 business days, the customer is entitled to a
  $10 service credit — apply it proactively, do not wait for the customer to ask.

## 4. Plan tiers

| Tier         | Monthly | Data      | Support channel priority           |
| ------------ | ------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Acme Lite    | $19     | 5 GB      | Standard queue                     |
| Acme Plus    | $39     | 50 GB     | Priority queue                     |
| Acme Max     | $69     | Unlimited | Priority queue + dedicated line    |

- Acme Max customers get an extended refund window of **45 days** on devices.

## 5. Escalation matrix

| Situation                                         | Escalate to             | Target response |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | --------------- |
| Refund request outside policy window              | Tier 2 — Billing         | 24 h             |
| Confirmed outage above 48 h                        | Network Operations       | 4 h              |
| Legal threat or regulator mention                  | Customer Advocacy team   | Same day         |
| Repeated SLA breach (2+ shipments, same account)   | Logistics supervisor     | 24 h             |

## 6. Agent conduct rules

- Never promise a refund outside the windows above; offer escalation to Tier 2 instead.
- Always cite the policy section you are applying (for example, "per section 1, Refunds").
- Do not disclose internal SLAs targets from section 5 verbatim; summarize them.
